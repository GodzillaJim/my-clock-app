# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Deep-work professionals: developers, writers, designers and researchers who protect long blocks of
concentrated work at a desk on Windows. Focus Sessions sits in the system tray; they start a session
(often from the keyboard or the tray), switch to their real work, and come back to the window only
to glance at time left, take a break, or look back at how their week went. Their attention is the
scarce resource; the app must never compete for it.

## Product Purpose

A desktop focus timer that turns "I want to work for two hours" into a plan of focus and break
segments, runs it reliably in the background (through sleep, crashes and restarts), and keeps an
honest local record of when and how well the person focused. Success is a person who forgets the app
is there while working, trusts it to tell them when to stop, and finds their history quietly useful.

## Brand Personality

Warm, encouraging, human. Like a supportive colleague who keeps time for you: it notices good work
and says so plainly, makes a missed or interrupted session feel ordinary rather than like failure,
and speaks in short, kind, specific sentences. Warmth comes from words, pacing and gentle
acknowledgement, not from decoration or rewards. Calm by default; never cute, never loud.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS dashboards**: card grids, big hero metrics, stat tiles everywhere.
- **Gamified productivity**: badges, confetti, points, streak fireworks, cartoon mascots. Progress is
  acknowledged, not rewarded.
- **The neon / glass "AI" look**: glassmorphism as decoration, glows, gradient text, purple-blue
  neon. (The current Aerogel style and the Deep Focus text glows drift here; the M4 design pass
  should bring both back in line.)
- **Cluttered Pomodoro apps**: many buttons, settings on the main screen, dense controls around the
  timer.

## Design Principles

1. **The timer is the product.** On the main screen nothing competes with the current session: one
   clear time, one obvious next action. Everything else is a step away.
2. **Warmth through words, not rewards.** Encourage with specific, human copy and gentle timing.
   No badges, confetti or scores; an interrupted session is just a shorter session.
3. **Stay out of the way.** Tray-first, keyboard-friendly, sensible defaults. The app should feel
   finished without opening Settings.
4. **Honest by design.** History shows what really happened (pauses and sleep never count as focus).
   Never inflate numbers to feel motivating.
5. **Calm in any light.** Comfortable for long stretches beside other work: low visual noise,
   respectful motion, readable at a glance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA (roadmap #63). Every control reachable and visible by keyboard, including while a
session runs; colour is never the only signal (focus vs break, completed vs interrupted); respect
`prefers-reduced-motion`; contrast holds in both light and dark styles; notifications and sounds
are optional and never the only cue.
